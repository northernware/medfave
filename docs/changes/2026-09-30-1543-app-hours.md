# Hours from the app

Opened: 2026-09-30 15:43 PHT

## What
- **`lib/hours.ts`:** saving a week (a doctor's, kept inside the clinic's;
  or the clinic's) moved out of `app/actions/schedule.ts` so the web and the
  app share it. The web's schedule actions now call it.
- **API:** `GET /api/v1/doctor/hours` → `{ mine, clinic }`; `PUT` with
  `{ which: "mine" | "clinic", days }`. `docs/api.md` updated.
- **CORS:** `PUT` added to the API's allowed methods (the app's web build
  needs it; native apps don't use CORS).

## Why
Mobile-first, step 3: doctors set their hours on the phone.

## Tested
- `tsc` and `eslint` pass. With medfave-mobile's `app-hours`: my hours
  load; Saturday past the clinic's closing is refused with the web's message;
  a valid change saves (DB: Monday 14:00); the clinic tab shows the clinic's week.
- The web schedule page was not re-run in a browser; its actions call the
  moved code unchanged.

## Not done / next
- Breaks, closures, service lengths, clinic details, inviting staff and
  sharing charts are still web-only.
- Reseeding a clinic leaves its old Doctor rows detached (clinicId null);
  harmless, but untidy in dev data.
