# Shared-charts setting and chart-opened log

Opened: 2026-09-30 14:46 PHT

## What
Phase 5, part 4 (medfave-design `plans/group-practice-findings.md`, decision 1).
- **`Clinic.sharedCharts`** (off by default). On: every doctor at the clinic
  reads every patient's chart and visit notes. Notes stay **read-only** to
  anyone but their author: no amend, archive or prescription printing.
  Switch in Manage → Clinic details (shown when a clinic has 2+ doctors).
  Plugs into `lib/care.ts` (`sharesCharts`, `caresFor`, `caredForIds`,
  `canReadNote`).
- **`ChartAccess` log:** every chart and visit note opened by a doctor is
  recorded (who, patient, note, when). The patient page shows "Who opened this
  chart" (last 12). A details-only view isn't a chart and isn't logged.
- Shared mode lists colleagues' notes on the patient page "by Dr. …", and the
  household page counts everyone's visits.
- **Privacy notice:** chart reading within a clinic, shared charts, and the
  log. `PRIVACY_NOTICE_VERSION` → `2026-09-30`.

## Why
Agreed decision: sharing charts is the clinic's choice, private by default,
and every chart opened is logged.

## Tested
