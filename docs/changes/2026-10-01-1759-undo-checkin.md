# Undo check-in

Opened: 2026-10-01 17:59 PHT

**What:** a checked-in visit can go back to Confirmed (`STATUS_TRANSITIONS`), labelled "Undo check-in" (`statusActionLabel`), until the consultation starts. Going back to Confirmed or Pending clears the arrival time (`queueStamps`). The desk's schedule panel offers "Undo check-in" on today's checked-in visits; appointment pages and the API (`nextStatuses`) list it too. Allowed for the desk.

**Why:** a mistaken check-in had no way back short of cancelling.

**Tested:** `tsc`, eslint.
