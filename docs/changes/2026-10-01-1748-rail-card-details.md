# Schedule timeline: room for each visit's details

Opened: 2026-10-01 17:48 PHT

**What:** 128px per hour (was 96). Each visit card shows the patient, the time range and service ("8:00 AM – 8:20 AM · Follow-up Checkup"), the reason, the doctor (on the desk, where every doctor's visits show), and the status or Check in / Start on its own line. Cards are at least 96px tall so a short visit still shows all of it; longer visits grow with their length.

**Why:** the user wanted more space so the appointment details fit.

**Tested:** `tsc`, eslint; Calendar screenshot (a 20-minute visit with all four lines).
