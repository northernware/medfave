# Permanent deletes look destructive; deleting an appointment asks for a typed phrase

Opened: 2026-10-02 15:47 PHT

## What
- `DangerZone` with `variant="danger"` (appointment, patient registration and household deletes) now looks like what it does: a red-tinted card with a warning icon and red heading, and the chevron at the right edge. Reversible archives and withdrawals keep the quiet style.
- New `confirmPhrase` prop and `TypedConfirm` (client): the button stays disabled until the phrase is typed. For an appointment it's **delete appointment**.
- The `deleteAppointment` action checks the phrase on the server too (`lib/confirm-phrase.ts`) and, if it's missing, sends you back with "Not deleted".

## Why
The user found the delete section out of place, not looking destructive, and wanted a typed phrase to confirm.

## Tested
`tsc` and `eslint` are clean. The phrase matching was run directly (exact, case and spaces ignored, wrong and missing refused). Not clicked through.

## Not done / next
- Patient registration and household deletes look destructive now but don't ask for a phrase. They can use `confirmPhrase` the same way.
