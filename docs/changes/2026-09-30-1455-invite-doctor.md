# Invite a doctor

Opened: 2026-09-30 14:55 PHT

## What
Phase 5, last part (medfave-design `plans/group-practice-findings.md`).
- **Staff → Invite** offers **Doctor** as well as Secretary and Administrator.
- **Accepting a doctor invitation** makes a DOCTOR member and a **pending**
  clinician profile, then lands on Manage with an **"Add your PRC license"**
  step (the resubmit form, now also for a first submission). They go into the
  `/admin/verify` queue once sent, and see no patients until verified.
- **Refused:** a doctor invitation to an email that already has a doctor
  profile or works at another clinic (several clinics is phase 6).
- **Admin queue** lists only doctors who have sent their license.
- **Gap closed:** `requireStaff` and `homeFor` send an unverified doctor in an
  already-open clinic to Manage (they could reach desk pages before).

## Why
The trial found a second doctor could only be added by the seed.

## Tested
