# A visit's page: two columns, tighter gaps

Opened: 2026-10-02 15:36 PHT

## What
- Doctor's visit page: the visit card and the delete section on the left; **Clinic use** and **History** in a 340px column on the right (wide screens). Gaps are 16 instead of 24.
- Desk's visit page: the visit and "Move it along" on the left, History on the right, 16 gaps.
- On phones both stay a single column, with the smaller gaps.

## Why
The user found the vertical space between sections on an appointment's page too big. Every card was stacked full width, 24 apart.
