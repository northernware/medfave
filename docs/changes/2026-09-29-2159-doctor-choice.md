# Every booking, request and schedule names a doctor

Opened: 2026-09-29 21:59 PHT

## What
Phase 5, part 1 (medfave-design `plans/group-practice-findings.md`). There is
no "the clinic's doctor" any more: `clinicDoctorId()` (first-created doctor)
is gone. New helpers in `lib/clinic.ts`: `clinicDoctors` (verified, by
name), `lastDoctorFor`, `pickDoctor` (asked-for → fallback → the only one).
- **Desk booking:** "Booking with" doctor picker (`components/doctor-picker.tsx`),
  preselected from the patient's last doctor; the desk sees **all** the
  clinic's patients (`clinicPatientOptions`) and the chosen doctor's hours.
  `bookAppointment` books into the chosen doctor's diary.
- **Desk registration:** a new household asks which doctor it's for (only with
  several doctors).
- **Schedules:** a doctor edits only their own; an administrator picks whose.
  Actions are bound to a doctor and re-checked. Manage lists every doctor's week.
- **Requests:** name a doctor (`doctorId`), defaulting to the patient's last
  doctor; checked against that doctor's hours. The portal has a doctor picker.
  A doctor's queue (API) shows only their own requests, and a doctor can
  accept/decline only their own; the desk sees and handles all, each labelled
  "For Dr. …".
- **API:** `GET /patient/clinic?doctor=` returns `doctors` and `doctorId`;
  `POST /patient/requests` takes `doctorId`; `/doctor/clinic` is the
  doctor's own hours. `docs/api.md` updated.

## Why
The test group practice showed the second doctor couldn't be booked, couldn't
set hours, and got nobody's requests. Decisions agreed with the owner.
