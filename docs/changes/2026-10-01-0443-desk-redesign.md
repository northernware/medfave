# Front desk redesign

Opened: 2026-10-01 04:43 PHT

**What:**
- New `components/app-shell.tsx`: the doctor's frame (sidebar on wide screens; on phones a slim bar with the logo, clinic line and an avatar to the account page, and the sections as pills). The doctor layout and the desk layout both use it now. `Nav` takes its links (`DOCTOR_LINKS`, `DESK_LINKS`, `CLINICAL_VIEW` for a doctor covering the desk).
- Desk Today: a fuchsia greeting header like the app's ("Good morning, Maria", date, Today / Waiting / Requests), and three big action tiles (Walk-in, Book, Add patient).
- Desk patient page: avatar and name header with full-width buttons on phones; Visits first, Medfave account and contact details beside them (under them on phones). "Issue activation code" is now "Link their account".
- Lists on phones: names and reasons wrap instead of being cut off; appointment badges sit under the name (`components/appointment-list.tsx`, shared with the doctor side). Answered requests: the name links to the booking.

**Why:** the user found the desk ugly, off-theme, different from the doctor side, and bad on phones.

**Tested:** `tsc`, eslint; screenshots at 390px and 1280px of Today, Appointments, Requests, Patients and a patient page, with no sideways scroll.

**Not done / next:** `/manage` (clinic settings) still has its own top header. Desk forms (book, walk-in, add patient) are unchanged.
