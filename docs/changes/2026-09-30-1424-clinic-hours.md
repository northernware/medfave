# Clinic opening hours; doctors' hours sit inside them

Opened: 2026-09-30 14:24 PHT

## What
Phase 5, part 2 (medfave-design `plans/group-practice-findings.md`, decision 4).
- New `ClinicOpeningHours` table: the clinic's own week. **No rows = no
  limit,** so existing clinics behave exactly as before until they set them.
- **Doctor hours must fit inside:** saving a day the clinic is shut, or times
  outside its hours, is refused per day ("The clinic is open 9:00 AM–12:00 PM
  on Saturdays", "The clinic is closed on Sundays").
- **Shrinking the clinic's hours** trims every doctor's bookable times
  (`withinClinicHours` in `loadSchedule`), and the schedule page names the
  doctors whose own hours now reach outside, to update.
- **Manage → Schedule:** "Clinic opening hours" card above each doctor's
  hours; Manage lists the clinic's week above the doctors'.
- **New clinics** get clinic hours at setup (Mon–Fri 9–5, Sat 9–12), the same
  as their doctor's. The group-practice seed sets Mon–Fri 8–5, Sat 9–12.
- Wider time inputs on the hours forms ("09:00 AM" was cut off).

## Why
Agreed decision: hours are per doctor, within the clinic's opening hours.
Online-consultation hours outside them come later, with teleconsults.
