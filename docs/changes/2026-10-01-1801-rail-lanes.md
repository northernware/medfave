# Schedule panel: overlapping visits side by side

Opened: 2026-10-01 18:01 PHT

**What:** visits whose cards would overlap on the timeline (same time, or back to back and shorter than a card) are laid out in lanes, side by side, splitting the width; a group ends when the next visit starts after every card above it. Narrower cards use slightly less side padding.

**Why:** stacked cards hid each other.

**Tested:** `tsc`, eslint; Calendar screenshot of Oct 8 (two visits at 10:00 side by side).
