# Start their own household

Opened: 2026-10-01 22:46 PHT

## What
- A desk chart now has a **Household** card. **Start their own household…** makes the patient head of a new household under their last name, and moves whichever housemates are ticked (a spouse, children) with them.
- It's a move, not a copy: one household per person, and records go with each chart. Anyone moving stops being the old household's point of contact. The new household's notes say "Started from the … household (Oct 2026)".
- "Open household" shows only for doctors, since that page is theirs.

## Why
The doctor asked about someone from a household starting their own family. Decision: move them to a new household rather than allow more than one (medfave-design PRODUCT.md, decision 3).
