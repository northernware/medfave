# Open sign-up: accounts, email verification, consent, rate limits

Opened: 2026-09-29 14:11 PHT

## What
Phase 1 of `medfave-design/plans/registration.md`, the web and API half.
- **Schema** (migration `20260929T0601_open_signup`):
  - `Account` gains `emailVerifiedAt`, `signupRole` (PATIENT/DOCTOR), `consentedAt` and `consentVersion`;
  - new `EmailVerification` and `RateLimitBucket` tables;
  - **every existing account is stamped verified** (a data step in the migration).
- **`/signup`:** name, email, password, "I'm a patient / I'm a doctor" (`?as=doctor` preselects it), and privacy consent. The account is signed in at once and lands on `/welcome`.
- **`/welcome`** (new): home for an account linked to nothing yet.
  - A "confirm your email" card with resend.
  - Patients get the activation-code form.
  - Doctors get a "set up your clinic — coming soon" card (phase 2).
- **`/verify-email`:** confirming takes a button press, because mail scanners open links and would use them up. Links are one-time, hashed, last 3 days, and a new one replaces the old.
- **`/privacy`:** a **draft** privacy notice (Data Privacy Act), versioned by `PRIVACY_NOTICE_VERSION`. It needs a lawyer's review and a named DPO before launch.
- **Rate limits** (`lib/rate-limit.ts`, database-backed): sign-in per email and per address, sign-up per address, code attempts, and reset or verification emails. The API answers 429.
- **API:** `POST /auth/signup`, `POST /auth/resend-verification`; `/me` adds `emailVerified` and `signupRole`.
- **Fixes:**
  - `/no-access` sat in the `(auth)` group, whose layout redirects signed-in users to `homeFor`, which was `/no-access`: a redirect loop for any account linked to nothing. It now redirects to `/welcome`.
  - Names and addresses in the password-reset and verification emails are now HTML-escaped, since names are user-chosen with open sign-up.
  - `src/prisma/db.ts` caches the dev client per schema hash, so a running dev server picks up a migration without a restart.
- The landing page CTAs are now "I'm a doctor" / "I'm a patient" to sign up, plus sign in.

## Why
The owner's registration plan: anyone can sign up as a patient or a doctor.

## Tested
- `tsc` and eslint pass; `db:verify` passes after `db:migrate` on the dev database.
- **API, 11 checks:**
  - existing accounts verified by the migration;
  - invalid fields, missing consent and a duplicate email are refused;
  - patient and doctor sign-up return `role: none`, unverified, and get 403 on patient and doctor endpoints;
  - resend works;
  - the 11th wrong sign-in for one email returns 429, even with the right password.
- **Browser:**
  - landing CTAs, then sign-up as a patient lands on `/welcome` with the confirm card and code form;
  - a verification link is confirmed by button, the database shows verified, and reusing the link fails;
  - `/welcome` stops asking; `/no-access` redirects to `/welcome`;
  - doctor sign-up via `?as=doctor` shows the doctor welcome; `/privacy` renders.
- No console errors.
- **Not tested:** actual email delivery (the test addresses don't receive mail).
