# Clinic QR code, poster, and Invite patients

Opened: 2026-10-01 03:21 PHT

## What
- **`/c/<slug>/qr`:** the clinic's QR code as a PNG (plum on white), opening
  its page. Public, like the page.
- **`/c/<slug>/poster`:** a printable poster (heart, clinic name, address,
  QR, "Scan to ask for a visit", link) with **Print poster**; always light.
- **Manage → Clinic details:** **Invite patients** (QR, link, Print poster,
  Download QR) is now separate from **Find a doctor** (the listing switch).
- `/doctor/clinic-settings` returns `slug` so the app loads the QR from the
  server it talks to. `docs/api.md` updated.

## Why
The owner wanted the link and a QR together under inviting patients, with a
downloadable/printable QR "like GCash", apart from the listing.

## Tested
- `tsc` and `eslint` pass. The QR PNG renders and scans as the clinic link;
  the Clinic details card and the poster show it (headless Chrome).

## Heads-up
- Links and QR codes use `APP_URL`; on Vercel it must be
  `https://medfave.vercel.app` or they point at localhost.
