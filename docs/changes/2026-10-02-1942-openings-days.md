# API: openings can list the next days with anything free

Opened: 2026-10-02 19:42 PHT

## What
- `GET /patient/clinic/openings` takes `limit` (max 60) and `perDay` (max 20). `perDay=1&limit=14` is the next 14 days with a free time. Documented in `docs/api.md`.

## Why
The app's Home now shows a day strip instead of time chips; times are picked in the request sheet.
