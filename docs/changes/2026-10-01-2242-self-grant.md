# Who can see my records: adults choose their own caregivers

Opened: 2026-10-01 22:42 PHT

## What
- `lib/caregivers.ts`: a patient sees who looks after their own chart. Adults (18 and over) can add someone by their Medfave email ("Let them look after me") and remove anyone, with no desk needed. A child's caregivers stay the desk's to change.
- A caregiver can **stop looking after** someone.
- Portal **My details**: a "Who can see my records" card, or "You look after …" with Stop when viewing someone else.
- API: `GET/POST /patient/carers`, `DELETE /patient/carers/:id` and `DELETE /patient/care`. `docs/api.md` is updated.

## Why
Adults consent for themselves ("Let Ana look after me"), and anyone whose records are seen by someone else should be able to see who.
