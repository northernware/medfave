# Check in on any day, with undo; visit history

Opened: 2026-10-02 04:00 PHT

## What
- **Check in, or start a consultation, on any day.** A visit booked for another day moves to now, so it's in today's queue. **Undo check-in** puts it back at its booked time, through the lock and clash check, since that slot may be gone. The schedule panel shows the buttons on every day again.
- New `AppointmentEvent` table: every status change and move, with who did it and when. It's written in `changeAppointmentStatus` and `startConsultation`.
- `lib/visit-history.ts`, and a **History** card on the doctor's and the desk's visit pages: requested (by whom) → accepted and booked (by whom) → checked in → consultation started → completed, including "Arrived on another day: moved from …". Older visits fall back to their stored arrival and start times.

## Why
The user asked for this: patients sometimes arrive before their booked date. Rather than a today-only gate, allow it and rely on undo. They also wanted a log of who requested, approved and booked a visit, and when it started.
