# Danger zone: a row and a dialog, not a tinted dropdown

Opened: 2026-10-02 15:51 PHT

## What
- `DangerZone` is now a client component: a plain row (what it does, and a **Delete appointment…** button) that opens a confirmation dialog (native `<dialog>`) with the warning, any extra fields, the typed phrase for a permanent deletion, and **Cancel**. The `<details>` dropdown and its red-tinted fill are gone; the card is neutral and red is kept for the buttons.
- A filled `dangerSolid` button variant, with an `--on-danger` text colour, is used for the final confirm. Archives and withdrawals use the neutral secondary button.
- A visit's delete section now sits last, full width, below both columns, apart from the visit.
- The danger red moves from hue 29 (a red-orange, #D02D23, with a peach tint) to hue 25 (about #C21725): `--danger`, `--danger-ink`, `--danger-soft` and `--danger-tint`, in light and dark. This affects every use of danger: errors, badges and the delete buttons.

## Why
The user found the previous version still bad: the placement, the dropdown, and the orange.

## Tested
`tsc` and `eslint` are clean. Not clicked through.

## Heads-up
The dialog needs JavaScript (the old disclosure didn't). Every other `DangerZone` (archive and delete on records, charts, households, requests) takes the new look.
