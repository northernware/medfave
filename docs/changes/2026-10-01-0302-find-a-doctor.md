# Find a doctor and new-patient requests (server)

Opened: 2026-10-01 03:02 PHT

## What
Phases 3–4 (plans/registration.md), server side.
- **Clinics:** `slug` (link name, made at setup and when listed) and
  `listed` (opt-in to "Find a doctor", off by default). Manage → Clinic
  details shows the link and a "List us" switch; the app's clinic settings API
  adds `listed` and `link`.
- **Public clinic page** `/c/<slug>`: the clinic and its verified doctors,
  "Open in the Medfave app" (`medfave://clinic/<slug>`) and sign-up.
- **Discovery API** (`lib/discovery.ts`): `GET /discover`,
  `/discover/doctors/:id`, `/discover/clinics/:slug`; verified doctors only.
- **New-patient requests:** `AppointmentRequest.patientId` is optional and a
  request can carry the patient's details (`new*` columns).
  `POST /discover/requests` sends one to any clinic; `GET /discover/requests`
  lists the account's requests everywhere.
- **Accepting a new patient's request** (desk web and the doctor's app API)
  asks for the record: **create** from their details (household by surname with
  their address, the doctor's), or **link** a look-alike found by
  `findPossibleDuplicates`. Their login is linked either way.
- Group-practice seed: the clinic is listed at `/c/magsaysay-group-clinic`.

## Why
The owner asked for both a clinic link/QR and in-app search; the patient side
of the app was empty apart from activation codes.
