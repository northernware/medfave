# App API: the three layers, and the chart for the app

Opened: 2026-09-30 15:08 PHT

## What
- **Privacy fix:** `GET /api/v1/doctor/patients/:id` returned every doctor's
  appointments with a patient (reasons included). It now returns only this
  doctor's own.
- The same endpoint follows `lib/care.ts`: `caresFor`, and the **chart**
  (allergies, alerts, conditions, medications, statuses) and **visits**
  (own; everyone's with `author` when the clinic shares charts) only when
  caring — else `null`. Opening the chart is logged (`ChartAccess`).
- `GET /api/v1/doctor/patients` adds `mine` per patient and `?who=mine`;
  the default stays the whole clinic (the booking picker needs it).
- `docs/api.md` updated.

## Why
Mobile-first: doctors should have the chart on their phone. The app API
predated phase 5's patient rules.

## Tested
- `tsc` and `eslint` pass. With medfave-mobile's `app-chart` PR, 10 checks
  on the group practice (see that note).

## Heads-up
- API: `/doctor/patients/:id` adds `caresFor`, `chart`, `visits`;
  `upcoming`/`past` are now the doctor's own appointments only.
