Opened: 2026-09-28 15:16 PHT · PR #14 (merged) · note written afterwards

PR #14 (merged). Written afterwards, when change notes began.

## What
- A public landing page at `/` (`app/page.tsx`) for signed-out visitors. Signed-in users are redirected to their home (`homeFor`).
- The doctor's dashboard moved from `/` to `/dashboard`. `homeFor`, the "Today" nav item, the 404 link, the desk and manage layouts, and the actions' `revalidatePath` calls all follow it.
- `components/brand.tsx` draws the approved lockup from medfave-design (path data in `components/logo-paths.ts`), coloured by `--logo-heart` / `--logo-word`.
- `AGENTS.md` points agents at medfave-design for UI work.

## Why
The site had no front door for visitors, and the logo was a stand-in.

## Tested
Typecheck and lint. `/` renders for visitors, and `/dashboard` redirects them to `/login`. Screenshots at desktop and phone width, light and dark. Not tested: a signed-in session.

## Not done / next
Nothing outstanding.
